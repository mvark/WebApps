tips

  • Use the dropdown to search by Title, Author, or Subject
  • Public access books are listed first, in bold. They can be read online for free
  • Books listed after them can be borrowed or have limited access. Borrowing usually needs a free Archive.org account
  • Archive.org links open the digitized copy of the book
  • Enclose the search keywords in quotation marks for an exact phrase search

Search Examples:

  • Title: "Pride and Prejudice", "Moby Dick", "Alice in Wonderland"
  • Author: "Jane Austen", "Charles Dickens", "Mark Twain"
  • Subject: fiction, science, history, poetry, philosophy

About Open Library:

Open Library is a project of the Internet Archive that provides access to millions of books. This search lists books with full text available through Archive.org.
